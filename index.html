FAQ

Questions people ask

Everything shop owners ask us before they start. Cannot find yours?

DL Still have a question? Talk to a real person on our team. Ask us
Do I need to install anything?

No. Dirav runs in the browser on a PC, laptop, tablet or phone. For the POS we help you connect your barcode scanner, receipt printer and cash drawer.

Can I move my existing products and customers?

Yes. Send us your Excel sheets or your old system export and our team imports your products, prices and customers during setup.

How long does setup take?

Most shops go live in about five days: demo, setup and data import, staff training, then your first bill.

What does the one-time setup fee cover?

Installation, importing your data, setting up printers and devices, connecting your webstore and training your staff.

Can I change my package later?

Yes. Start with POS or Webstore and move to POS + Webstore any time as your business grows.

Can I pay yearly?

Yes. Pay yearly and you get two months free on every package.

Is my data safe?

Your data is stored on secure cloud servers with daily backups, and every user has their own login and permissions.

Which hardware works with the POS?

Standard USB or Bluetooth barcode scanners, 58mm and 80mm thermal receipt printers, cash drawers and label printers. We can advise you before you buy.

How much does a POS system cost in Sri Lanka with Dirav Labs?

The POS package is LKR 15,000 one-time setup and LKR 3,000 a month. The Webstore package is the same price. POS + Webstore together is LKR 25,000 setup and LKR 2,500 a month. Pay yearly and you get two months free.
