FAQ

Questions people ask

Do I need to install anything?

No. Dirav ERP runs in the browser, on a PC, tablet or phone. For POS we can help you connect a barcode scanner and receipt printer.

Can I move my existing products and customers?

Yes. Send us your Excel sheets and our team imports them during setup.

Is my data safe?

Your data is stored on secure cloud servers with daily backups, and every user has their own login and permissions.

Can I change my package later?

Yes. You can upgrade or add modules at any time as your business grows.
